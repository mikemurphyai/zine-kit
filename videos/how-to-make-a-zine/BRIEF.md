## Intent

A how-to explainer: "How To Make A Zine". Fill in the 8 pages in the One-Sheet Zine Kit,
print one landscape US Letter sheet, then Task 1 (make the fold lines), Task 2 (cut the
slot, with its CAUTION), Task 3 (fold the zine through the "+" shape). All narration and
on-screen text is written in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English: imperative mood,
one instruction per sentence, procedural sentences of 20 words or fewer, approved words
only ("open" not "unfold", "fold line" not "crease", "make sure" not "ensure"), no
phrasal verbs, no "-ing" verbs, safety notice before its step (CAUTION = risk of damage,
command first then reason), NOTE = information only.

## Assets

- ../../zine-kit.html — the kit itself; source of the STE task text and the design tokens.
- ../../README.md — the sheet layout and the STE rules.

- Voiceover reads the key step of each action; the full STE step list shows on screen as

- On-screen captions on.
- Sound bed: soft, light acoustic, low under the voice. No SFX.
- Visuals: drawn fold diagrams of the sheet (8 panels, top row upside down, orange cut
  line, slot opening into a diamond, "+" shape). No site capture.
- End card shows zine.imurph.com.
