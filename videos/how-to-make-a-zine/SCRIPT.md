**Time:** 32.0 – 47.0s
**Delivery:** Even, one instruction per breath.

## Line 7 — Task 2: Cut the slot (Frame 7)

**Time:** 47.0 – 62.0s
**Delivery:** Steady; stress CAUTION and pause after it.

    Task two. Cut the slot. Fold the sheet in half from left to right. Caution: do not cut past the first vertical fold line. If the cut is too long, the zine will come apart. Cut along the horizontal fold line from the folded edge.

## Line 8 — Task 3: Fold the zine (Frame 8)

**Time:** 62.0 – 78.0s
**Delivery:** Even, one instruction per breath.

    Task three. Fold the zine. Fold the top row back, behind the bottom row. Hold the two ends. Push them slowly to the center. The slot opens into a diamond, then into a plus shape. Fold the four arms together to close the zine like a book.
