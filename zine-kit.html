Fold and cut the zine

Written in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English

TASK 1

Make the fold lines

  1. Put the sheet flat, with the printed side up.
  2. Turn the sheet so that the long edges are at the top and the bottom.
  3. Fold the bottom edge up to the top edge.
  4. Push your thumbnail along the fold.
  5. Open the sheet.
  6. Fold the left edge to the right edge.
  7. Fold the sheet in half again. Put the folded edge on the open edges.
  8. Push your thumbnail along each fold.
  9. Open the sheet fully.
  10. Make sure that you have 8 equal panels, in 2 rows of 4.
TASK 2

Cut the slot

  1. Fold the sheet so that the left edge touches the right edge.
  2. Hold the sheet with the folded edge on the left.

CAUTIONDo not cut past the first vertical fold line. If the cut is too long, the zine will come apart.

  1. Cut along the horizontal fold line from the folded edge.
  2. Stop the cut at the first vertical fold line. The cut is 2.75 in (70 mm) long.
  3. Open the sheet.
  4. Make sure that the slot is on the cut line between the 2 center panels of each row.
TASK 3

Fold the zine

  1. Fold the top row back, behind the bottom row.
  2. Make sure that you can see the front cover and pages 6, 7, and 8.
  3. Hold the left end and the right end of the sheet.
  4. Push the two ends slowly toward the center. The slot opens into a diamond shape.
  5. Continue to push until the sheet makes a "+" shape.
  6. Fold the 4 arms together to close the zine like a book.
  7. Put the front cover on the outer side.
  8. Push along the spine to make the folds flat.
  9. Make sure that the pages are in the sequence 1 to 8.

NOTEUse only one side of the sheet. The other side is on the inner side of the zine. To make more copies, copy the flat sheet before you cut it.
